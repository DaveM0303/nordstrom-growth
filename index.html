How I Manage Your Media Dollars

I don’t start with ad spend. I start with the business behind it.

Every dollar has to earn the next one. Before scaling spend, I look at what is selling, what can support more demand, what the economics allow, and what the performance data is actually telling us.

01
AssortmentIdentify hero SKUs, seasonal opportunities and products with the strongest potential to absorb incremental demand.
02
InventoryCheck inventory depth and replenishment before increasing spend. There is little value in scaling a product that is about to stock out.
03
KPI TargetsTrack ROAS, conversion rate, CPC, CTR, AOV and product-level economics so every campaign has a clear performance threshold.
04
Bidding & BudgetAdjust bids and budget based on demand, conversion, inventory and profitability—not simply traffic volume.
05
Scale WinnersIncrease investment behind products proving they can convert while monitoring inventory and diminishing returns.
06
Cut Wasted SpendReduce, pause or rebuild campaigns and products that consistently miss their performance thresholds.
